# Areev Brand Guidelines

**Version 2 — adopted 2026-08-22.** This file is the source of truth for the
Areev brand. It supersedes the mono-blue palette previously documented in
`../areev-marketing/visuals-guide.md` and the old brand section of `CLAUDE.md`.

> Note on the wordmark typeface: the generated logo was rendered from a
> rounded geometric sans-serif description rather than a specific font file.
> For a reproducible brand system, Areev is standardized on **Sora**;
> **Sora Medium (500)** is the closest fit to the `reev` letterforms —
> technical, geometric, modern, but not overly futuristic.

## Brand

**Areev**
**Self-improving agents, governed.**

Areev is the substrate for adaptive agents — agents that improve from their own history, under human authority, through steps that can be inspected, undone, and re-measured.

The Areev identity represents continuous improvement without uncontrolled autonomy.

---

## Logo

The primary Areev logo combines three elements:

**A** — Areev itself, represented by a distinctive custom letterform.

**reev** — the wordmark, set in a clean geometric typeface.

**Improvement Loop** — a clockwise loop surrounding `reev`, representing continuous learning, evaluation, and improvement.

The loop returns toward the **A**, reinforcing an important principle of Areev:

> Improvement happens continuously, but remains governed.

The **A used in the wordmark is also the Areev icon**. A separate or alternative "A" symbol should never be created.

---

## Logo Meaning

The visual system reflects the Areev improvement cycle:

**History → Observe → Propose → Govern → Apply → Measure → Improve**

The clockwise loop represents iteration and self-improvement.

The return of the arrow toward the A represents governance, accountability, and the ability to bring every improvement back under human authority.

The loop is intentionally open rather than a completely closed circle. Areev does not imply uncontrolled autonomous self-modification.

---

## Primary Logo

The preferred logo consists of:

**Blue custom A + dark `reev` + blue clockwise improvement loop**

Use the primary version on white or very light backgrounds whenever possible.

The logo should remain horizontal.

Do not separate the loop from `reev` when using the full wordmark.

---

## Areev Icon

The standalone **A** is the official Areev icon.

Use it for:

- GitHub organization avatars
- Favicons
- App icons
- Social avatars
- Small interface elements
- Documentation icons

The icon must use the **same exact A geometry as the primary wordmark**.

Do not create a simplified, alternate, or stylistically different A.

## Typography

### Brand Typeface

**Sora**

Primary source: Google Fonts.

### Logo Wordmark

**Sora Medium — 500**

Use Sora Medium as the reference typography for `reev`.

Letter spacing should remain neutral or slightly tight.

The wordmark should feel:

- geometric
- confident
- technical
- approachable
- precise

Avoid excessively bold weights.

### Product and Website Typography

| Use | Font | Weight |
|-----|------|--------|
| Headings | Sora SemiBold | 600 |
| Subheadings | Sora Medium | 500 |
| Body | Sora Regular | 400 |
| Labels / UI | Sora Medium | 500 |

For code, terminals, benchmarks, and developer-facing technical content, use a dedicated monospace font such as:

**IBM Plex Mono** or **JetBrains Mono**

## Logo Geometry

The A, wordmark, and improvement loop should behave as one visual system.

### A

The A should be slightly taller and visually stronger than the lowercase wordmark.

Its bottom baseline should align optically with the bottom boundary of the improvement loop.

The A should maintain its custom internal cut and rounded geometric construction.

### Improvement Loop

The loop travels clockwise.

It begins at the upper-left of `reev`, travels horizontally across the top, turns down at the right, travels across the bottom, and terminates with an arrow pointing diagonally toward the upper-left and toward the A.

The upper-left start and lower-left arrow termination should be visually balanced and symmetrical.

Both should use subtle curves rather than hard ninety-degree corners.

The top and bottom portions of the loop should maintain equal visual distance from the wordmark.

The right-side upper and lower corner radii should match.

The loop stroke should remain consistent throughout.

---

## Clear Space

Always leave sufficient space around the logo.

Minimum clear space should be approximately equal to the width of one main stroke of the A on every side.

No text, interface element, border, illustration, or other logo should enter this area.

More whitespace is preferred whenever possible.

---

## Minimum Size

For the complete Areev wordmark:

**Digital:** minimum approximately 120 px wide.

Below this size, use the standalone A icon.

For favicons or very small interface elements, use only the A.

---

## Light Background

Preferred treatment:

- A and loop: Areev Blue
- `reev`: Areev Ink
- Background: White

This is the primary brand configuration.

---

## Dark Background

For dark interfaces:

- A and loop: Areev Blue
- `reev`: White
- Background: Areev Ink or another approved dark neutral

Ensure sufficient contrast.

Do not place the dark `reev` wordmark directly on dark backgrounds.

---

## Monochrome Logo

When color reproduction is unavailable, the complete logo may be rendered entirely in:

**Black** on light backgrounds

or

**White** on dark backgrounds.

Do not introduce arbitrary monochrome colors.

---

## Incorrect Usage

Do not:

- replace the custom A with another A
- use a separate A icon unrelated to the wordmark
- change the arrow direction
- reverse the improvement loop
- distort or stretch the logo
- rotate the logo
- alter the relationship between A and `reev`
- change individual letter colors
- use neon blue or cyan substitutions
- add shadows, bevels, outlines, or 3D effects
- apply gradients unless an official gradient is later defined
- place the logo on visually noisy backgrounds
- change the arrowhead into a decorative symbol
- close the loop into a generic circular refresh icon

---

## Brand Personality

Areev should feel:

**Technical, not futuristic for its own sake.**
**Powerful, but governed.**
**Adaptive, but inspectable.**
**Modern, but credible.**
**Precise, not decorative.**

The visual identity should communicate that Areev enables systems to improve while preserving human authority, evidence, reversibility, and measurement.

---

## Brand Principle

**Agents improve. Humans remain in authority.**

Every use of the Areev identity should reinforce this relationship between adaptation and governance.
