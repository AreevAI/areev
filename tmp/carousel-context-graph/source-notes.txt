Editorial guardrails:
- Context graph is an emerging, non-standard term. Define the intended operational meaning in the carousel.
- Do not say knowledge graphs are static; they can carry time, provenance, and evolving facts.
- Present the context graph as an activation/assembly layer over durable knowledge, not as a second graph database.
- Do not equate observable decision traces with private model chain-of-thought.
